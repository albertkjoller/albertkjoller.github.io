This is a personal academic website run by {{ site.first_name }} {{ site.last_name }}. If you have questions about this page, use the contact links on the [about page]({{ '/' | relative_url }}).

**No cookies, no tracking.** This site does not set cookies and does not track you across websites.

**Visitor statistics.** To see roughly how many people visit and from which countries, the site uses [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com), a privacy-friendly analytics service. It counts page views without cookies and without storing personal data: your IP address is only used to look up your country and is not stored, and visitors are counted with a hash that changes daily, so visits cannot be linked to you or across days. See [GoatCounter's privacy policy](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy) for details.

**Third-party resources.** Like most websites, pages load some files (fonts, scripts for math and figures) from external servers, such as Google Fonts and the jsDelivr and cdnjs content-delivery networks. Your browser contacts these servers directly, which means they receive your IP address as part of the request. The site is hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/about-github-pages#data-collection), which may log visitor IP addresses for security purposes.

**Links.** Links to other websites (papers, code, social media) are not covered by this page; those sites have their own privacy policies.
